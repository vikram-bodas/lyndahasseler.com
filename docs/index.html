Welcome

Thank you for being part of the music

For thirty-five years I have had the joy of making music with the singers of Capital University, in the Chapel Choir and Choral Union, at the Christmas Festival, and in more rehearsals than I could ever count. Whether you sang with us, taught alongside me, or listened from the audience, you are part of that story.

As one chapter closes, this site is a place to stay connected. Over time it will grow into an archive of the music we made together and a home for the teaching ideas behind it.

With gratitude,

Lynda

We'll laugh, we'll cry, we'll chase beauty, and we'll heal hearts, including our own.

Lynda Hasseler

About

Dr. Lynda R. Hasseler

Dr. Lynda R. Hasseler is Director of Choral Activities at Capital University's Conservatory of Music in Columbus, Ohio. For thirty-five years she has led the Chapel Choir and the Choral Union, and conducted the university's beloved Christmas Festival in Mees Hall.

Under her direction, the Chapel Choir earned a gold medal at the World Choir Games, performed at conferences of the American Choral Directors Association, and gave world premieres of new choral works.
